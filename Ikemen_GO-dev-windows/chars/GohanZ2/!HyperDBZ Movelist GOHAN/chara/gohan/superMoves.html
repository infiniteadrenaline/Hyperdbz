Masenko (LV1)
Gohan's famous attack is a short-range energy wave that draws the enemy in upon hitting them. For zoning prowess, Gohan is able to launch the Masenko diagonally upwards. When in Unleashed Mode, the Masenko is changed to its former chargeble, electric projectile glory. The longer you charge it up, the longer it dizzies the opponent when it hits them.
Soaring Dragon Strike (LV1)
This Super move has Gohan dashing across the screen at high speed and leaping up with an enormous uppercut as soon as he reaches the opponent, hitting multiple times and sending them flying high into the air. You can dash-cancel out of the Strike after it ends hitting the opponent.
Thunder Flurry Kick (LV1)
Aerial rapid kick that halts Gohan's momentum and hits multiple times. This replaces his Super Hurricane Kick from prior versions. It's able to suck in the opponent if they're near and moreso if they're hit once, they're pulled in. Good combo-ender. You're able to supercancel into it during either of his Hurricane Kick special moves as well as his Diving Kick Flurry.
Masenko (LV2)
Souped-up version which fires a larger spark, this one reaches across the screen. The Unleashed version likewise is souped-up.
Soaring Dragon Strike (LV2)
Souped-up with a faster dash and more hits and damage! Also comes with a big aura surrounding his arm so you know Gohan means business! You can dash-cancel out of the Strike once it starts hitting the opponent.
Super Thunder Flurry Kick (LV2)
This boosted-up version of the Thunder Flurry Kick has Gohan traveling forward as he performs the rapid kick, and ending with a Bicycle Kick that causes a ground-bounce.
Gekiretsu Ranbu (LV3)
Gohan's Ultimate is a versitile counter move. If he's hit during his special pose, Gohan counters the opponent and destroys him with an explosive beatdown. However the attack can also be comboed into at the cost of slightly lower damage. Also, in Unleashed mode, Gohan gains access to different finishers, which are performed automatically depending on the Unleashed Bar level;
Lv1 - Hi-Ex Blast Fist.
Lv2 - Demon Tribe Beatdown.
Lv3 - Flash Kamehameha.
The Flash Kamehameha plays after performing the combo-version of the Gekiretsu Ranbu. However, if you're at Level 3 of the Unleashed Bar and manage to land the raw counter against the enemy, a wholly different cinematic plays out, so definitely give that a try!
