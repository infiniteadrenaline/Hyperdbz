activate Grudgeless Mode (LV2)
Activating Grudeless Mode makes Golden Freeza a bit more dangerous and ferocious!
-Gain 'You'll Find Me To Be Elusive' (detailed below).
-Gain 'My Hand Slipped' (detailed below).
-While you have a Red Flame active, the damage of every non-flame Special is increased with 10%.
-While you have a Blue Flame active, connecting with any non-flame Special eats away the opponent's Super Bar.
-The "If I Can't Win, I'll Destroy Everything!" LV3 move now only costs LV2 to perform and adds Finishers at the end of it depending on what Grudgeless level you are:
LV1 - Inescapable Death Web.
LV2 - Might Of The Supreme.
LV3 - Miraculous Limitbreaker.
activate 'You'll Find Me To Be Elusive' (aka 'Boss Counter')
This is a heavy reference to the previous AI-only Golden Freeza boss, which had a 'feature' which allowed him to break out of combos after the 7th consecutive hit of one. In this iteration, it's an install of sorts, and he'll remain in this install for a number of seconds. If at any point while this is active, he is hit at least 3 times consecutively he'll use his old trick of teleporting out of the combo. A usage or getting hit also ends the install. The availability of these boss counters are indicated by the 'F's on his special power bar. You can only use two per Power Mode install. Whether you use one, both or none, you get them back at the start of the next round if you are still in Power Mode.
'My Hand Slipped' (aka 'Dashing Orb Plant')
Freeza does a short dash forward (whether you use the Backward or Forward key+buttons) which acts as a grab-attempt. The move itself is a hit-grab. If Freeza succeeds in grabbing the opponent, he'll plant a purple psychic time-bomb on them, which detonates after a while. When it does, it launches the opponent into the air. When you succeed in grabbing the opponent with the back+MP+MK variant, he'll teleport behind them switching sides and then plant the bomb. When you grab the opponent another time when the first bomb hasn't detonated yet, it'll instead refresh the timer. The bomb will explode when a Hell Flame Special hits the opponent as well. But the orb will dissolve from the opponent if Freeza is hit, doing no damage.
'I Won't Allow It!' (aka 'Revenger Outburst')
An unconventional Power Mode exit attack. Freeza will launch a surprise energy beam after he's knocked down. You've got to input the command when you're lying down after a normal or hard knockdown.
