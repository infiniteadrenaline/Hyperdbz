Death Blaster (LV1)
Golden Freeza launches a large energy blast which knocks the opponent back.
Nova Crash (LV1)
No ifs or buts; Freeza powers-up then charges forward, crashing into the opponent hard.
|
Spider Death Beam (LV2)
A Death Beam is fired out of each of Freeza's fingers (minus his thumbs), creating a spider web of sorts. Once the opponent is hit by this, another series of Death Beams rain down on them from various directions.
Die By My Hand (LV2)
*Note: You need to have two Red Hell Flames active before you can activate this attack.*
Golden Freeza fires a devastating energy beam diagonally upwards. If the opponent is caught by this, they get pummeled by a boat-load more hits as the beam keeps dealing them.
Wicked Storm (LV2)
*Note: You need to have two Blue Hell Flames active before you can activate this attack.*
Golden Freeza conjures up four levitating rocks. These will nudge forward along the direction of Freeza's physical attacks, with the goal of them hitting the opponent. After a while, they crumble away.
If I Can't Win, I'll Destroy Everything! (LV3)
Freeza channels all of his power through the ground, creating a shockwave that can knock the opponent on their rear-end. Then, a magmafied pillar erupts in front of him. The opponent is carried up by this if they hit it. When performed whilst Power Mode is active, this will lead to Finishers.
