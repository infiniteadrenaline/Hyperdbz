Activating Sadism Mode makes Freeza a bit more dangerous and ferocious!
- Upon activation you will see an empty Sadism Bar (divided into 3 sections for LV1, LV2 and LV3) and a red kanji added to the screen.
- Hitting the opponent fills up the Sadism Bar.
- Freeza gains access to the Teleport Dash, Telekinetic Rock Throw and Death Charge (detailed below).
- You gain a 'starter set' of rocks for the Telekenitic Rock Throw (detailed below).
- Tail Press Nightmare costs LV2 instead of LV3 and also cancels the mode.
Teleport Dash
During Sadism Mode, Freeza gains a short-range teleport. The forward version can only be done when he's close enough to the opponent, and when doing so he'll switch sides with them.
Telekinetic Rock Throw
Freeza shows off, using telekinesis to levitate a group of six small rocks around him.
Pressing LP fires a single rock forward. Upon hitting the opponent, it does absolutely minute damage but counts as a genuine hit with hitstun and thus can be used to extend combos. Press forward+LP to fire a single rock forward faster than with neutral LP. You cannot fire out a rock if you're holding Back on the directional pad. Also note that hitting with a Single Rock takes a way a little bit of the Sadism bar.
Inputting QCF+K with at least one rock still present makes you fire ALL remaining rocks across the screen. LK fires them straight forward, MK diagonally upwards and SK vertially up. All of these rock throwing moves can be performed while in the air as well, but the directions of the MK and SK versions will be swapped (firing them down, not up). Unlike the Single Rock, hitting the opponent with All Rocks will fill up your Sadism bar. Once you've ran out of rocks, you can perform the activation command again and start over.

Death Charge
A stronger version of the Head Charge based off Freeza's Desperation Move in DBZ Hyper Dimension. He charges forward with his arms in front, surrounded by energy. It takes its power from the Sadism Bar, so the more that Bar is filled, the more damaging the move will become.
